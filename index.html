WELCOME TO

PM SHRI Upgraded High School

PM SHRI Upgraded High School Bariyatu is a premier co-educational institution dedicated to developing confident, compassionate and responsible global citizens. We blend academic excellence with character building to prepare students for a successful future.

Selected under the Government of India's prestigious PM SHRI initiative, our school stands as an exemplar center of high quality, joyful, inclusive, and skill-oriented learning from Classes I to X in Gola block, Ramgarh.

Sri Jitendra Kumar Mahto

Principal / Head of Institution
M.A. (TRL) NET M.A. (Economics) M.A. (Education) NET B.Ed (JTET)
Education is not merely about textbooks and examinations. It is about building character, instilling values, and preparing young minds to become responsible citizens of this great nation. At PM SHRI Upgraded High School Bariyatu, every child matters, and every dream is valued.
Our Vision
“To provide every student with quality, inclusive, joyful and skill-based education, enabling them to become responsible, self-reliant, creative and contributing citizens who play an active role in nation-building.”
