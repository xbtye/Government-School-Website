PM SHRI Upgraded High School (UHS) Bariyatu

PM SHRI Upgraded High School Bariyatu (PM SHRI UHS Bariyatu Gola) is a premier co-educational Government School located in Bariyatu Gola, Ramgarh, Jharkhand. Dedicated to developing confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizens, our institution blends academic excellence with character building to prepare students for a brighter tomorrow.

Selected under the Government of India's prestigious PM SHRI (Pradhan Mantri Schools for Rising India) initiative, UHS Bariyatu Gola stands as a model Government School providing high-quality, joyful, inclusive, and skill-oriented learning from Class 1 to Class 10 in Gola block, Ramgarh district, Jharkhand.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PM SHRI Upgraded High School (UHS) Bariyatu, Gola, Ramgarh, Jharkhand.

PM SHRI UHS Bariyatu Gola (PM SHRI Upgraded High School Bariyatu) is a prestigious Government School located in Bariyatu, Gola Block, Ramgarh District, Jharkhand. It has been selected under the Government of India's PM SHRI flagship initiative to deliver modern, 21st-century quality education.

Yes, PM SHRI School Bariyatu Gola is a recognized Government School (Government School Bariyatu Gola) under the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, supported by the Central Government's PM SHRI scheme.

UHS Bariyatu Gola is located at Bariyatu Gola, Ramgarh, Jharkhand - 829110. You can find our exact location on Google Maps under the listing Upgrade High School Bariyatu with direct road access.

PM SHRI Upgraded High School Bariyatu Gola provides co-educational schooling from Class 1 to Class 10 (Primary, Upper Primary, and Secondary), following the JAC (Jharkhand Academic Council) curriculum integrated with NEP 2020 experiential learning.

Our school offers interactive digital smart classrooms, high-tech ICT computer labs, science experimentation kits, a well-stocked library, safe RO drinking water, nutritious Mid-Day Meals, sports coaching, and girls self-defence training.
